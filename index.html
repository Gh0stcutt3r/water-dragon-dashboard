Launch Report Card

Grades a new launch's first week against every launch old enough to judge: first sales Oct 01 to Oct 19, 2026, 81 launches, outcomes read today. Alive means a sale within the last 7 days, fading means quiet 7 to 13 days, dead means quiet 14+ days (the Graveyard rule). Figures are medians per group. Tape only: OpenSea sales on tracked Robinhood Chain collections. One exclusion: the yield-deed instrument, which is not a collectible launch. Repeat-token share is the share of week-1 sales that were resales of a token already sold that same week, the sign of a secondary market forming.

Base rates: of 81 launches, 62 are alive (77%), 6 are fading, and 13 are dead, roughly three to five weeks after launch.

Survivor floor = the level 75% of survivors cleared. Grade a launch against it, not against the median.

What separated them

Breadth beat money. Survivors drew about five times as many distinct buyers in week 1 as the launches that died, while week-1 ETH volume was about the same for both. At this market's scale, volume is a vanity metric; buyer count is the vital sign.

A secondary market formed fast, or not at all. Among survivors, about 30% of week-1 sales were tokens changing hands a second time within the week. Among the dead, about 3%. If nobody is reselling your tokens in week 1, week 4 is usually quiet.

The grade: clear the survivor floor on buyers, sales, and repeat share and a launch is tracking with the ones that lived. Miss on buyers and the rest rarely saves it.

How to read this

Tiers are 1k supply bands: sub-1k, 1k-2k, 2k-3k, 3k-5k, 5k-6k, 6k-7k, 7k-8k, 8k-9k, 9k-10k, 10k+ (split from a single 5k-10k band on 2026-10-25). Floor changes are ETH-denominated collections only, measured against each collection's own latest snapshot. A collection with no live floor counts as dead for this read. Small n means wide uncertainty: treat thin tiers as hints, not verdicts.

What the Dragon means

Every label the dashboard uses, defined exactly as the code computes it. Thresholds come from the v0.4 config, if a number changes there, this page follows.

Data sources

  • OpenSea, collection snapshots (floors, owners, volume): last pulled Nov 9, 2026, 8:48 AM EST
  • OpenSea, sales tape: last pulled Nov 9, 2026, 8:58 AM EST
  • OpenSea, top-30 universe refresh: last pulled Nov 9, 2026, 8:47 AM EST

Timestamps show when each pull last ran, not when the last sale happened. A quiet tape still gets pulled on schedule.

Tape

Every individual sale — price, buyer, seller, timestamp, tx hash. The source of truth. Every detector, score, and chart derives from the tape, never from floor snapshots alone.

Tape depth

How many days of history we hold for a collection: first observed sale, else first snapshot, else when it entered the tracker. This is how long we've watched, not the project's true age. A short tape means week-over-week reads are partial — direction, not verdict.

Floor

The lowest currently listed price. A lagging snapshot: useful context, but a flat floor can hide real movement on the tape.

Price unit

Sale prices are recorded as ETH only when the trade was denominated in ETH or WETH. Trades in other tokens (e.g. USDG) are tagged with their symbol and excluded from ETH volume and price aggregates — never silently converted.

Dive ≤ 7 days from first sale

A buy within 7 days of a collection's first observed sale. Measures early conviction — not necessarily smart conviction.

Dive win

An dive where the wallet later sold the same collection above its average buy price.

Dive rate min 2 dives

Dive wins ÷ dives. Wallets need at least 2 dives to appear on the leaderboard — one lucky buy is not a track record.

Realized PnL

Total sell revenue minus total buy cost (ETH), counting only trades visible on the tape. Understates true PnL — off-tape and cross-chain trades are invisible.

Sweep ≥ 5 buys / 6h

5 or more sales to a single buyer within 6 hours. Flags buyer concentration. Sweeps are often followed by dumps — the pattern, not the buyer, is the signal.

Whale

We don't score wallet size — we detect whale behavior. A wallet moving size, not a wallet holding size. See Whale dump.

Whale dump ≥ 3 sells / 24h

One wallet selling 3 or more tokens of a single collection within 24 hours. Possible distribution, panic exit, or rotation — the tape shows the what, not the why.

Wash flag confidence 0.60

Wallets A and B trading with each other in both directions on one collection. Confidence 0.60. Investigative only — a flag is never an accusation.

Volume spike ≥ 3× 7d avg

24-hour sales at or above 3× the trailing-7-day daily average. Momentum, for better or worse.

♘ Trojan horse 1–72h gap

A sweep (apparent demand) followed by a dump from the same wallet, 1–72 hours later. The gift that was an exit — buying pressure as camouflage for distribution. The Wood Horse watches the gates for these.

Mint-out planned

Time from mint open to sellout. Defined in the v0.4 spec — detector not yet built.

Attribution methods manual

How wallets get tied to teams — the playbook from the CCFF00 ecosystem audit (Oct 2026), the standard we hold manual investigations to. Strongest first: constructor arguments and payment-splitter releases (named on-chain); first-funder tracing with public bridge solvers filtered out; shared exchange deposit addresses; SeaDrop drop-settings comparison (payout wallet, fee recipient, signer); metadata hosts; repeat OpenSea counterparties; bridge-origin decoding; behavior fingerprints (gas settings, nonce patterns, time-of-day). On-chain patterns show what happened, not intent — a money link is not a team tie.

Cohort: mint-comps

Free-mint comparables for your launch: Cartoonals Classics, City of Stonks. The lens for your mint-date decision.

Cohort: broad

Everything else. Feeds wallet intelligence (breadth is the feature) and regime reads.

Regime

Market-wide direction from aggregate tape: volume trend plus per-collection price direction. Answers 'is the water rising or falling', not 'should I buy'.

Survival curve accruing

Probability a collection's floor sits at or above mint price at day 30 / 60 / 90 / 180. Spec'd in v0.4 — the dataset is still accruing toward it.
